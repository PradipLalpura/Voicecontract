## 01 — THE NAME & ITS ORIGIN

ANTARIK is not a made-up word. It carries weight that precedes us.

Derived from Sanskrit **Antarikṣa** (अन्तरिक्ष) — meaning *space, atmosphere, the region between earth and sky* — the name encodes what we do and why we do it.

The space between earth and sky is not empty. It is the medium through which light travels, sound resonates, weather forms and breaks, and signals reach their destination.

This is the operating metaphor. We are not the earth (the brand) and we are not the sky (the audience). We are the atmosphere — the engineered medium that makes transmission possible.

**"We are not the destination. We are the atmosphere that makes the signal land."**

## 05 — VISUAL IDENTITY

### The Two Themes

ANTARIK has two official color themes. Both are equally canonical. Neither is a fallback.

**Light Theme** is the atmosphere at noon — open, clear, direct. Used in presentations, documents, client-facing materials, and light-mode digital products.

**Dark Theme** is the atmosphere before dawn — deep, intelligent, precise. Used in dark-mode digital products, developer-facing tools, technical documentation, and premium digital experiences.

The accent colors — Signal Cyan, Craft Gold, Violet — are **identical in both themes**. They are the signature. They never change. Only the surfaces change around them.

---

### LIGHT THEME — Atmosphere at Noon

Bright. Grounded. Distinct. Colors that feel alive in daylight, legible on any device, premium without being precious.

#### Light Theme Color Architecture

| Name | Hex | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sky White** | `#F7F9FC` | Soft blue-white | Primary background. Breathing room. The canvas. |
| **Stone** | `#EEE9E0` | Warm off-white | Secondary background — cards, sections, elevated surfaces. |
| **Deep Navy** | `#1A2744` | Rich dark blue | Primary text. Headlines. Anchors. Authority. |
| **Muted Steel** | `#6B7A99` | Blue-grey | Secondary text, labels, metadata. Never primary. |
| **Signal Cyan** | `#00C2CC` | Electric teal | **Signature accent.** CTAs, links, highlights, active states. |
| **Craft Gold** | `#E8C547` | Warm yellow-gold | Moments of completion. Premium badges. Used sparingly. |
| **Violet** | `#7B5CE5` | Medium purple | AI, automation, and tech-specific contexts. Secondary interactive. |

#### Light Theme Color Pairing Rules
- Sky White background + Deep Navy text: the default combination
- Signal Cyan on Deep Navy: high contrast, high-signal moments
- Craft Gold on Deep Navy: earned moments only — completion states, premium callouts
- Violet on Sky White: AI and tech features specifically
- Stone as card background on Sky White: creates depth without darkness
- Never: dark void backgrounds as the primary surface in light theme

---

### DARK THEME — Atmosphere Before Dawn

Deep. Precise. Calm. A sky that carries signal without noise. Every color earns its brightness because the ground it stands on is deep.

Not dramatic for its own sake. Not moody. The same brand — thinking at night.

#### Dark Theme Color Architecture

| Name | Hex | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Void** | `#080E1A` | Deep navy-black | Primary background. The atmosphere. The canvas. |
| **Surface** | `#0F1825` | Elevated deep blue | Cards, panels, elevated sections. First layer above void. |
| **Layer** | `#162038` | Lighter deep blue | Modals, hover states, active cards. Second layer. |
| **Border** | `#1E2D45` | Subtle navy border | Card borders, dividers. Barely visible. |
| **Border Active** | `#2A3F60` | Visible navy border | Focused inputs, selected states, active borders. |
| **Starlight** | `#EDF2FF` | Soft blue-white | Primary text. Not pure white — atmosphere in it. |
| **Dusk** | `#7A8BA6` | Muted blue-grey | Secondary text, labels, metadata. Dimmed but readable. |
| **Horizon** | `#3D4F66` | Deep slate | Tertiary text. Placeholders. Disabled states. |
| **Signal Cyan** | `#00C2CC` | Electric teal | **Signature accent.** Identical to light theme. CTAs, links, highlights. |
| **Craft Gold** | `#E8C547` | Warm yellow-gold | **Identical to light theme.** Glows on dark. Earned moments. |
| **Violet** | `#7B5CE5` | Medium purple | **Identical to light theme.** AI, tech, automation contexts. |

#### Dark Theme Color Pairing Rules
- Void background + Starlight text: the default combination
- Signal Cyan on Void or Surface: precise, clean, unmissable
- Craft Gold on Surface or Layer: glows like light through atmosphere — use sparingly
- Violet on Surface: AI and tech feature contexts
- Surface cards on Void background: subtle depth, not dramatic
- Layer modals on Surface: layered depth without harshness
- Never: white or near-white backgrounds in dark theme — not even for cards
- Never: pure black (#000000) as the background — Void is dark, not absent

#### Dark Theme — The Palette in Context

```
Page background       →  Void       #080E1A

Modal / Elevated      →  Layer      #162038

Primary text          →  Starlight  #EDF2FF
Secondary text        →  Dusk       #7A8BA6
Tertiary / disabled   →  Horizon    #3D4F66

Signature accent      →  Cyan       #00C2CC

```

### Typography

Three typefaces. Each with a role. Never crossed. Identical in both themes.

| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | Editorial New Italic | Hero text, section titles, brand moments. Italic only. |
| **Metadata / Labels / Code** | JetBrains Mono | Tags, timestamps, code snippets, technical labels. |
| **Body / UI** | DM Sans | All running text, UI elements, descriptions, captions. |

**Typographic Rules — Light Theme**
- Headlines: Large, confident, italic Editorial New. Deep Navy or Signal Cyan.
- Body: DM Sans Regular, line-height 1.6+. Deep Navy (`#1A2744`).
- Labels: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, small, Muted Steel (`#6B7A99`).

**Typographic Rules — Dark Theme**
- Headlines: Large, confident, italic Editorial New. Starlight or Signal Cyan.
- Body: DM Sans Regular, line-height 1.6+. Starlight (`#EDF2FF`).
- Labels: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, small, Dusk (`#7A8BA6`).

---

### Motion Principles

Motion exists to transmit meaning. Never to decorate. Identical in both themes.

- **Entrances**: Elements arrive with purpose. Clean fade + subtle upward drift. Duration: 200–400ms.
- **Transitions**: Eased. Never bouncing. Never spinning.
- **Hover states**: Subtle cyan shift on interactive elements.
- **Page transitions**: Fast cross-fades. Lenis smooth scroll on web.
- **Dark theme hover**: Surface elevates to Layer on hover — no color change needed, just depth.
- **Rule**: If removing the animation loses no information, remove it.
