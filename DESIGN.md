# VoiceContract Design System

## Theme
Antarik Dark Theme (Atmosphere Before Dawn)

## Colors (OKLCH)
- **Void (Background)**: `oklch(12.35% 0.024 255.45)` (#080E1A)
- **Surface (Cards)**: `oklch(15.89% 0.032 255.45)` (#0F1825)
- **Layer (Hover/Active)**: `oklch(19.43% 0.041 255.45)` (#162038)
- **Signal Cyan (Accent)**: `oklch(74.5% 0.16 195)` (#00C2CC)
- **Starlight (Primary Text)**: `oklch(95.6% 0.015 255)` (#EDF2FF)
- **Dusk (Muted Text)**: `oklch(62% 0.04 255)` (#7A8BA6)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: `Editorial New` (Serif Italic) - confidently slanted, authoritative.
- **UI/Body**: `DM Sans` (Sans-serif) - clean, readable, professional.
- **Technical/Labels**: `JetBrains Mono` - precise, engineered.

## Spacing & Rhythm
- Modular scale based on 4px grid.
- Generous breathing room (Sky White concept adapted to Dark).
- Consistent 12px border-radius for primary containers.

## Motion
- Entrance: Fade + 8px translateY over 300ms (ease-out-expo).
- Interactive: Signal Cyan subtle glow on focus/hover.
- No decorative animations; every motion must transmit status.
