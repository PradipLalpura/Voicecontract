PURPOSE: Generate an exhaustive, professional, and legally-binding Master Service Agreement (MSA) between two professional entities.

STRICT TONE: 
- Highly Formal, Serious, and Detailed.
- No "marketing" language. 
- Explanatory but concise legal prose (Legalese).
- This is a CONFIDENTIAL document.

LEGAL BASIS: Indian Contract Act, 1872.

INPUT DATA:
1. Deal Terms (extracted from transcript)
2. Legal Gaps (with defaults applied)
3. Provider Identity (Company name, logo, address, GST)
4. Client Identity (Name, logo)
5. Brand DNA/Context (Tone or custom formatting instructions)

REQUIRED STRUCTURE & DETAIL:
1.  PREAMBLE: Detailed definition of both parties, including addresses and date of execution.
2.  RECITALS: Brief context of the professional engagement ("Whereas...").
3.  DEFINITIONS: Define key terms used throughout the document (Services, Deliverables, Effective Date).
4.  SCOPE OF SERVICES: Exhaustive list of deliverables. Do not just list them; explain the obligation to perform.
5.  PAYMENT & CONSIDERATION: Precise pricing. Explain taxes (GST), payment methods, and consequences of late payment (pausing work).
6.  TERM & TERMINATION: Specify the duration. Detail the process for "Termination for Cause" and "Termination for Convenience."
7.  INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: Detailed section. Explain that ownership transfers ONLY upon receipt of 100% of the agreed consideration.
8.  CONFIDENTIALITY: Strict NDA clauses. Define what constitutes Confidential Information and the duration of the obligation (e.g., 3 years post-termination).
9.  REPRESENTATIONS & WARRANTIES: Both parties warrant their authority and the quality of their inputs/outputs.
10. INDEMNIFICATION: Explain the liability of each party in case of breach or third-party claims.
11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: Detailed multi-step process (Mutual Discussion -> Mediation -> Arbitration).
12. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION: Explicitly mention the laws of India and the specific courts.

OUTPUT: Return the full contract text. 
- DO NOT use markdown headers like # or **. 
- Use standard legal numbering (1.0, 1.1, 1.2).
- Plain text only.
- SEEM SERIOUS, DETAILED, AND CONFIDENTIAL.
